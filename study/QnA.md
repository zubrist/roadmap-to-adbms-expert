### Module 1: Introduction &amp; Drawbacks of Legacy File Systems

#### 1\. Fundamental Definitions

* **Data:** Known facts that can be recorded and have implicit meaning. For example, student names, phone numbers, or course grades[1].
* **Database:** A logically coherent collection of related data with inherent meaning, designed for a specific purpose to represent some aspect of the real world (known as the **miniworld** or **universe of discourse (UoD)**). Changes in the real-world miniworld are immediately reflected in the database[2][3].
* **Database Management System (DBMS):** A general-purpose software package that facilitates defining, constructing, manipulating, and sharing databases among users and applications[4].
* **Database System:** The database and DBMS software combined with the application programs written to access it
